## Verdict key

- **ACCEPT:** the exact proposal is continuity-safe enough to advance to author
  review; it is not thereby accepted into the manuscript.
- **REVISE:** the identified issue is valid, but the proposed wording would create or
  preserve a canon/voice problem. A narrower alternative is supplied.
- **REJECT:** do not advance this proposal; the existing text or scene function is
  stronger/safer.
- **AUTHOR DECISION:** evidence does not settle the underlying world rule.

defense. At line 10966 and in the website/context lock, the meaningful choreography
is the fixed sequence intent, courage, law, resonance, shadow, record. Both can be
true only if one of these models is intended:

1. The six-domain sequence is fixed for this class of decision, while other decision
   classes have different authorized sequences; or
2. Order is dynamically selected, and this scene’s sequence is one valid ordering,
   not the constitutional default.

The website must not teach a universal fixed order until Issac chooses. The current
page concept may present this chapter’s order as a useful decision worksheet, not as
the only valid form of in-world attestation.

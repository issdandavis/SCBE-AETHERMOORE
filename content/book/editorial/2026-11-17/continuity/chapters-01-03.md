The best-supported direction is to keep the published order, repair Chapter 2’s form/awakening handoff, and accept W07’s timing correction. Issac must approve that bridge.

## Required revisions before a candidate patch

### W05 — preserve the four semantic definitions

The safe structure is:

> His state vector--nine semantic dimensions the Protocol tracked continuously. He could resolve only four: his context window (...), temporal phase (...), entropy level (...), and quantum coherence (...). All nine numbers updated in real time...

Do not replace the definitions of context window, temporal phase, entropy, and coherence with names alone. This scene is the Reader Edition’s first and only local explanation of what those dimensions mean. The exact remaining five dimensions are not supplied here and should not be invented to make the list feel complete.

### W06 — submit the full compressed counterpoint

The second pass may be shorter, but the continuity keeper must be able to inspect all six proposed definitions together. Canon locks:

- KO remains outcome/control/declared intent, not implementation.
- AV remains transport, routing, initialization, and channel establishment; it does not independently own permission or concealment.
- RU remains policy, access, permission, conditions, and exceptions.
- CA remains computation, encryption, transformation, processing, and complex spellwork.
- UM remains security, privacy, concealment, protection, and the risk of hiding from Protocol verification.
- DR remains schema, structure, authentication, data integrity, signature, and proof.

The first sensory circuit at `ch02.md:L74-L87` must not be compressed. The second circuit’s job is shared vocabulary and banter, so remove repeated glosses only after preserving the RU door, CA recognition, UM warning, Izack/Polly feather tell, DR receipt, and network-stack realization.

### W11 — keep drift, not correction, as the compounding quantity

A continuity-safe version of the proposed ordering would be:

> “Tiny intent drift,” Polly said. “The room makes drift visible as distance, then makes distance expensive. Harmonic cost. In bounded systems, drift is annoying. In hyperbolic systems, each bit of drift compounds the next.”

This remains optional prose. The important ruling is that the revision must not state or imply that corrective action itself necessarily increases harmonic cost.

## Revision stance

These chapters already complete a strong three-step movement: verify a living relationship, demonstrate readiness inside changing conditions, then make a distributed decision. The revision should repair contradictions and frame breaks, trim only where repeated explanation delays an emotional turn, and leave the memorable sensory and domestic landings intact.

Priority order:

1. Resolve the Sera-role, Council-seat, “Cast Bible,” evidentiary-certainty, and vote/witness inconsistencies.
2. Repair stale standalone chapter numbering at the generation authority.
3. Have the author decide whether Dremsreach returns the forty-second hand delay or leaves a persistent tax.
4. Consider density and pace edits only after the canon repairs; reject any optional trim that weakens Marcus’s humor or the scenes’ emotional breathing room.

### Chapter 23 — The Underroot

#### W23.1 — Keep Sera Voss’s office consistent

**Location:** line 10291  
**Priority:** high; canon continuity

**Before**

> “Voss’s sister,” Marcus said. “Sera. Third ring relay technician. You knew her.”

**After**

> “Voss’s sister,” Marcus said. “Sera. Third Circle Runethic. You knew her.”

**Value:** Voss identifies Sera as a Third Circle Runethic at line 10241, and Marcus repeats that office in Chapter 25 at line 10814. **Risk:** If “Third ring relay technician” is an intentional former office or additional credential, the line should name that relationship rather than silently replacing it. The author should confirm Sera’s canonical title before acceptance.

#### W23.2 — Honor Senna’s pass/warrant distinction

**Locations:** lines 10209, 10251, 10273  
**Priority:** medium; terminology consistency

Senna explicitly corrects “corridor pass” to “corridor warrant” at line 10087 because a pass implies possession. Later narration and dialogue return to “pass.”

**Before**

> The corridor pass had failed for five independent reasons...

> ...stealing a corridor pass doesn’t make the problem false.

> That is why the corridor pass system exists.

**After**

> The corridor warrant had failed for five independent reasons...

> ...stealing a corridor warrant doesn’t make the problem false.

> That is why the corridor warrant system exists.

**Value:** The live-proof concept depends on authority being renewed through relationship rather than carried as property. Keeping Marcus, narration, and Kelth on “warrant” pays the correction instead of treating it as a disposable joke. Chapter 25 already says Voss stole a corridor warrant.

**Risk:** If “corridor pass system” is the formal institutional name and “warrant” only Senna’s preferred precision, retain the formal term and make that distinction explicit elsewhere. Do not normalize terminology until the continuity keeper checks other regions.

#### W23.3 — Preserve humane quarantine without erasing its teeth

**Location:** line 10227  
**Priority:** medium; moral and physical precision

**Before**

> Marcus looked at the thief. The man sat with his back against root-wall, in a space that had been designed to hold him without hurting him, caught by a system that had treated his failure as a medical event rather than a crime -- quarantine, not punishment.

**After**

> Marcus looked at the thief. The man sat with his back against root-wall, in a space that had tightened to hold him without yet hurting him, caught by a system that had treated his failure as a medical event rather than a crime -- quarantine, not punishment.

**Value:** “Tightened” describes what the scene showed; “yet” preserves the fact that Voss surrendered under real pressure. The Underroot can be humane because it assesses before punishing without becoming harmless or sentimental.

**Risk:** “Yet” makes injury sound like the system’s eventual plan. If corridor mercy guarantees noninjury, reject this edit and establish the safety invariant explicitly. The intended failure state belongs to the author.

#### W23.4 — Optional compression after Voss’s emotional peak

**Location:** line 10263  
**Priority:** low; pacing only

**Before**

> Marcus watched the intake process and recognized the bureaucratic cousin of every corridor warrant he’d carried that day. Every step documented. Every variable recorded. Voss’s actual burden, intent, and route history mapped against the stolen warrant’s declared values, producing a discrepancy report that read like a structural damage assessment: here is what the corridor expected, here is what it got, here is the stress the difference placed on the local root-grid, here is what would have happened if the quarantine had not engaged.

## One cinematic website candy concept

### “The Living Decision” — a free Verify / Understand / Witness field card

A single, approximately ninety-second page experience gives the visitor a complete useful pattern without requiring the novel:

1. **Verify — root floor:** wet stone and pale-gold rootlight respond to scroll or keyboard steps. Six small signals—identity, pace, purpose, burden, witness, changing ground—stay coherent only while context stays connected. A copied token goes gray. Plain-language gift: **proof is a living relationship, not a possession.**
2. **Understand — phase tide:** the same route cycles through iron mist and frost. The visitor cannot force the shortest path; pausing reveals a warm herepoint. Plain-language gift: **readiness is understanding demonstrated under consequence.**
3. **Witness — governance hall:** six restrained color bands answer in order: Why act? What risk must be faced? What rule binds us? What must work together? What hidden cost remains? What will be recorded? The final decision is visibly composed from all six answers. Plain-language gift: **a trustworthy decision has distinct witnesses, not one louder voice.**

The free artifact is a downloadable one-page **Living Decision Card** with spaces for live context, readiness evidence, the six questions, a named maintainer, and the next check. No email, confession, payment, or lore knowledge is required. The final frame is not a larger spectacle: worn boots beside a paper receipt in human-scale amber light, with clear delivery and support terms. The book and an audit service are honest continuations, not prerequisites for receiving the method.

Accessibility and canon guardrails: reduced-motion mode freezes each scene into a readable illustration; all meaning exists in text and focus order; resonance is captioned rather than audio-dependent; no generated real person appears; page language is an analogy, not a newly named in-world protocol.

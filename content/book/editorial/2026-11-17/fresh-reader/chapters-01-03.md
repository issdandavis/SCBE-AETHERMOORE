## Chapter 1 — “Protocol Handshake”

### Unprimed reaction

**[OBSERVATION]** I trusted Marcus before I understood the world. The open window, Bryce’s photo, the bass guitar, expired yogurt, and dead coffee made his exhaustion feel like grief with an engineer’s work habits rather than generic “burned-out protagonist” shorthand (`ch01.md:L4-L20`). The first hard lock-in was the valid token that should not exist (`L22-L40`). From the whiteout onward, the chapter kept changing registers—security mystery, bodily dislocation, archive wonder, bird comedy, existential countdown—without losing Marcus’s diagnostic voice (`L46-L72`, `L76-L94`, `L98-L166`). Polly made the impossible world feel safe enough to enter without making it safe.

### The free piece of candy

**[OBSERVATION]** The chapter gives the reader a compact question with personal utility: **“What do you intend?”** It is answered not by grand purpose but by warm air, a friend’s photograph, gratitude, and the wish to understand (`L56-L64`). The emotional reward is equally small and human: after the threat and spectacle, Polly promises warmth and a mug (`L192-L208`).

**[INFERENCE]** A public page can give this away as a thirty-second **Protocol Handshake**: the visitor chooses one honest intent—understand, build, verify, protect, or explore—and receives one plain-language route through the site plus one useful artifact. It works without novel knowledge because it is simply a humane navigation question. No account, lore quiz, or purchase gate should precede the result.

### Attention curve

- **[OBSERVATION — rises]** The “valid but impossible” tokens and unauthorized hallway create the first mystery with unusually clean stakes (`L22-L40`).
- **[OBSERVATION — rises sharply]** Reality asks a question, listens to a specific human memory, and routes Marcus like a packet (`L46-L72`). This is the chapter’s signature fusion of feeling and systems language.
- **[OBSERVATION — rises and relaxes]** Polly’s academic regalia, translation, fear for Marcus, and casual transformation turn exposition into relationship (`L98-L166`).
- **[OBSERVATION — rises]** The first outside view supplies scale, while the coffee exchange restores human scale (`L172-L208`).
- **[OBSERVATION — drops slightly]** The apartment, Thursday departure, mattress, expired yogurt, and not-going-home facts recur within `L10-L20`. On a first pass I momentarily felt the opening restating its evidence before the log anomaly arrived.
- **[OBSERVATION — confuses briefly]** `L42` shifts from past narration into “but how could he not? It is the taste of his actual life.” The tense change read like a surface inconsistency rather than a deliberate internal-thought marker.
- **[INFERENCE]** “Isekai’d” at `L128` will delight readers who share the vocabulary and may momentarily puncture the spell for readers who do not. The surrounding joke supplies enough meaning that it is not a comprehension blocker.

### Desire to continue

**[OBSERVATION]** Three independent pulls remain active: the seventy-two-hour existence deadline (`L138-L143`), Polly’s fear and care (`L132-L158`, `L202-L208`), and the structured unauthorized message Marcus recognizes at the end (`L212-L216`). I continued for the relationship first and the system mystery second.

### Canon, emotional, and visual motifs

- **[OBSERVATION — canon]** Intent is beneath wording and is tested for honesty (`L56-L64`).
- **[OBSERVATION — emotional]** Familiar rituals—window air, a photograph, coffee, a mug—carry more weight than spectacle (`L10-L18`, `L192-L208`).
- **[OBSERVATION — visual]** Black/deep-violet feathers, obsidian eyes, crystal light, academic regalia, violet-gold sky, and pale-blue routes form the immediate palette (`L102-L116`, `L170-L184`).

### Standalone page-experience concept

**[INFERENCE]** **Handshake / The Warm Route.** Begin with a blinking cursor and the question “What do you intend?” A visitor’s selection sends one slow pale-azure route through crystal-like structure to a real destination: a book sample, technical proof, project demonstration, or contact route. The experience remains intelligible as thoughtful site navigation even if the visitor never learns who Marcus or Polly is.

### The free piece of candy

**[OBSERVATION]** The six tongues form a useful real-world checklist: intent, route, permission, computation, protection, and proof (`L74-L87`, `L148-L175`). The gift is not the invented vocabulary by itself; it is a memorable way to ask whether a system has separated concerns it usually muddles together.

**[INFERENCE]** A page can give visitors a **Six-Voice System Check**: six colored facets, each with one plain question—What should happen? How does it travel? Who may do it? What transforms it? What stays hidden? What proves it happened? The output can be a downloadable six-line review card. This is useful to a technical visitor without requiring belief in magic or familiarity with the novel.

## Chapter 3 — “Hyperbolic Consequences”

### Unprimed reaction

**[OBSERVATION]** This was the most immediately playable chapter. Essence brew pays off the coffee promise and lets Marcus and Polly be funny before training begins (`ch03.md:L4-L52`). The accidental chair turns “honest intent beats stated wording” into a visual gag I could understand instantly (`L112-L146`). The RU denial then makes the abstraction bodily: reduced resolution, a skipped heartbeat, and Polly moving closer (`L162-L204`). By the end, the basement distress signal turns the original unauthorized channel into a voice asking for help (`L336-L380`).

### The free piece of candy

**[OBSERVATION]** The chapter gives a practical rule: clean wording cannot rescue conflicted intent, and small drift becomes costly when a system compounds it (`L114-L156`, `L220-L238`). It also gives a compassionate corollary: another person’s chosen proximity can help stabilize you (`L188-L194`).

**[INFERENCE]** A page can offer a **Clarity Before Cost** mini-tool. The visitor states an outcome, then checks the hidden need or constraint underneath it. The page returns a gentle mismatch—its signature example can be an ordinary chair—plus a concise prompt to clarify before committing money or compute. This works as a useful planning exercise without any lore explanation.

### Attention curve

- **[OBSERVATION — rises]** Essence-brew banter immediately converts promised comfort into chemistry and character (`L4-L52`).
- **[OBSERVATION — dips slightly]** The terrace panorama at `L56-L70` is attractive but closely follows the extended world panorama near the end of Chapter 2 (`ch02.md:L200-L220`), so it felt like a second arrival before the training room supplied a new problem.
- **[OBSERVATION — highest rise]** The chair misfire, successful ring opening, and policy denial form a clean comedy-to-danger escalation (`L112-L204`).

- **[OBSERVATION — rises sharply]** Bilingual logs, many voices, red DR sigil, and timed knocks provide the strongest end hook in this set (`L336-L380`).
- **[OBSERVATION — spatial confusion]** At `L192`, Polly is described as being on the “table edge,” although the training hall established an obsidian plinth and later an accidental chair, not a table (`L72-L86`, `L132-L148`).
- **[OBSERVATION — surface confusion]** The nested dialogue quotes at `L248-L250` and `L276` are not escaped/curled in Markdown, which interrupted the reading surface.
- **[INFERENCE]** “I intend to understand, and stay” at `L302` is emotionally appealing but reads like a major choice very soon after arrival. A fresh reader may interpret “stay” as “remain through the danger” or “live here permanently”; the intended scope is not yet clear.

### Desire to continue

**[OBSERVATION]** The distress signal is the decisive trigger (`L336-L380`). It promises that the strange traffic from Chapter 1, the missing seventh domain from Chapter 2, and the hidden deep layer belong to the same problem. Polly’s “call for me” instruction (`L308-L324`) also creates an immediate relational question: will Marcus seek help or investigate alone?

### Canon, emotional, and visual motifs

- **[OBSERVATION — canon]** Hyperbolic space makes drift compound; literal systems make vagueness expensive (`L220-L238`).
- **[OBSERVATION — emotional]** Comfort is calibrated but still domestic: a large warm mug, food, sleep, a blanket, and someone nearby (`L4-L26`, `L258-L306`).
- **[OBSERVATION — emotional/canon]** Presence behaves like social verification: Polly moves within six inches and Marcus stabilizes faster (`L188-L194`; echoed by `interlude-01-pollys-vigil.md:L102-L120`).
- **[OBSERVATION — visual]** Layered dawn rings, living rune-trees, a room breathing out of square, amber apertures, red denial glyphs, and warm half-shadow provide cinematic beats with human-scale anchors (`L56-L84`, `L152-L180`, `L324-L368`).
- **[OBSERVATION — theme]** “That’s not architecture. That’s parenthood” explicitly turns world-building into care for something that continues growing (`L60-L66`).
- **[INFERENCE]** The page feeling is: **precision can be kind because consequences are real; support is part of system integrity**.

### Standalone page-experience concept

**[INFERENCE]** **The Honest Chair.** A small, accessible intent sandbox begins in a warm refectory palette, asks for one desired action and one current need, and visualizes agreement as a stable amber aperture. Mismatch produces a harmless chair and explains why. A second panel shows one route bending as assumptions accumulate, with a plain-language “clarify before this becomes expensive” takeaway. The visitor leaves with a copyable intent/constraint statement; the paid continuation can be the book or a deeper technical service, never the explanation required to understand the gift.

# Exact-coordinate triadic sidecar benchmark

The first triadic experiment still decoded evidence coordinates through the
learned route core. That created gradient interference: role supervision reduced
byte-exact decoding to about ten percent and one seed's route reasoning floor to
0.15.

This repair reads signed bit coordinates directly from each lossless form. All
six forms vote on each coordinate. Aligned tongue and ordinary exact-byte arms
must agree exactly; shuffled and random controls retain their disagreement as a
fractional coordinate. The sidecar is detached from autograd.

The affirm, counter, and judge heads operate only on this coordinate receipt.
Semantic gradients update the six evidence scales and the judge, while route,
cycle, identity, and residual gradients update the neural path. This tests the
architectural claim that a realm can make decisions without deforming the
shared staircase that transported it there.

The five equal-parameter arms and three-seed gates match the first triadic run.
The traceability gate requires every aligned supervised seed to score at least
0.90 on held-out truth, stance, reason, minority evidence, and contribution
sign. Separate gates compare role supervision and aligned versus ordinary and
shuffled representations.

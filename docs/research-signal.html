Tongue integrity · 02

Different null spaces make a stronger gate

An exact six-axis residual named its complete blind direction. The bijective tongue braid then rejected a representative corruption on every blind pair.

59,913 cases

Measurement details

Across 64 opcodes, the residual detected 100% of 378 single-tongue and 99.68% of 59,535 two-tongue corruptions. Its 189 misses were exactly equal shifts on KO↔CA, AV↔UM, or RU↔DR. The cage localized both dissenting tongues for a +7 probe on each pair. Read the evidence note.

Exact finite residual proof · three cage probes · no claim beyond the configured 64-opcode tongue roads

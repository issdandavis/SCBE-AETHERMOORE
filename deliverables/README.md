# deliverables/

Customer-facing bundles.

`SCBE_Production_Pack/` is the sold product pack: a buyer start guide, delivery and access notes, a machine-readable `PACKAGE_INVENTORY.json`, and four packages under `packages/`:

- `ai-governance-toolkit/` — decision-record template, threshold worksheet, pilot checklist.
- `content-spin-engine/` — source-to-channel matrix and transformation patterns.
- `hydra-agent-templates/` — role templates, packet example, launch checklist.
- `n8n-workflow-pack/` — n8n workflows with connector and environment-variable notes.

These are templates and checklists, not executable software.
